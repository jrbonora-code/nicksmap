## Delivered: the OZ / EB-5 rural screen

`scripts/oz-eb5-screen.mjs` (new) with four modes — `tracts`, `sites`, `parcels` and `assets` — documented in [OZ-EB5-SCREEN.md](OZ-EB5-SCREEN.md). No application code, dependency, migration, environment variable or Railway state changed. Outputs and cached downloads live in the Git-ignored `.local/oz-eb5-screen/`, with products at the root plus `raw/`, `import/`, `tools/` and `runs/`, and a README there. The five-layer data hierarchy (tracts, markets, sites, assets, leads) in [OZ-EB5-SCREEN.md](OZ-EB5-SCREEN.md#data-hierarchy) is the rule for adding any further source: it joins an existing layer on its key or it is not added.

Layer 4 is now a mode rather than a pair of helper tools. `node scripts/oz-eb5-screen.mjs assets` joins three anonymous HUD-hosted property layers to the tract table by 2010 tract ID and writes `oz-eb5-assets.csv`, `.xlsx` and a manifest with page receipts, reconciliation against each layer's own count, and a `by_county` block for the Markets layer: 19,822 rows in designated tracts, 4,912 of them in rural_clear across 898 counties (HUD-assisted 978, USDA rural housing 1,738, LIHTC allocation records 2,196). It reproduces the two retired one-off tools figure for figure; those tools and their CSVs are kept as evidence in `runs/before-assets-mode/`. Layer 1 gained five appended columns — `oz2027_eligible_land_pct`, `oz2027_status`, `oz2027_rural_land_pct`, `oz2027_rural_flag`, `oz2027_basis` — from a fresh receipted download of the IRS Rev. Proc. 2026-14 appendix (25,332 eligible tracts, 8,334 OZ-rural), bridged from 2020 to 2010 boundaries by land share; that appendix flag is the OZ 2.0 rural definition and is never substituted for EB-5 rural, and every pre-existing column and class count was proved unchanged by diff.

Layer 1 then gained ten more appended columns, the three incentive flags: HUD's 2026 LIHTC Qualified Census Tracts (14,496 tracts) and 2026 Difficult Development Areas (2,902 records), both read from their FeatureServer layers with a receipt per page, and the CDFI Fund's 2016-2020 ACS New Markets Tax Credit Low-Income Community eligibility file (85,395 tracts, 35,335 eligible), downloaded with a receipt. The two tract-keyed lists are bridged from 2020 to 2010 boundaries by land share exactly as the 2027 columns are, on the same thresholds. Of the 1,614 rural_clear tracts, 383 are wholly QCT and 196 partly, 287 are in a non-metropolitan DDA, and 1,179 are wholly NMTC-eligible with 190 partly. Every pre-existing column, every class count and `oz-eb5-markets.csv` were proved unchanged by diff, and three tracts per flag were re-checked against the source.

Layer 1 then gained its last planned addition on September 18, 2026: eleven ACS demographic columns, the same three facts at two dates. **At designation** (`acs2015_*`) is the 2011-2015 ACS read from the CDFI Fund's own 2011-2015 low-income-community file, which is on 2010 tracts and joins directly on the GEOID with no bridge; all 8,764 designated tracts join it, 8,488 met the low-income test the designations rested on and 276 did not (contiguity or another route). **Latest** (`acs2024_*`) is the ACS 2020-2024 5-year release read from the Census table-based summary files for B01003, B17001 and B19113, keyless, on 2020 tracts, and carried onto a 2010 tract only where containment makes the figure exact: counts are summed only when every successor lies wholly inside the tract, a median is carried only from a single successor, and nothing is apportioned by land share. That rule leaves 4,771 tracts `whole` (837 rural_clear), 705 with a `containing_tract` reading of the rate and median only, 3,140 `partial` with nothing carried, and 148 `unknown`. The Census Data API needs a free key and was not used; every source here is keyless. Every pre-existing column, every class count and `oz-eb5-markets.csv` were proved unchanged by diff, and the values were spot-checked against the sources with independent parsers.

Three pieces are deliberately not answered rather than guessed. The **metropolitan DDA leg** is not implemented: metropolitan DDAs are designated by ZIP Code Tabulation Area rather than by county or tract, so the 6,796 metropolitan tracts read `metro_zcta_not_evaluated` and answering them needs a tract-to-ZCTA crosswalk. **Connecticut** is `unknown` for both the QCT flag and the latest ACS on all 72 of its designated tracts, because HUD and the 2020-2024 ACS both code the state on the 2022 planning regions while the Census relationship file still keys it on the 2020 counties; one planning-region tract crosswalk would answer both. The **island territories** (75 designated tracts) are outside the ACS and read `unknown` for the latest figures with that reason. A population-weighted crosswalk would let the 3,140 `partial` tracts carry latest figures; that is a possible addition, not a planned one, and it would replace exact figures with modelled ones.

The preparer is now parser `oz-eb5-import-2`: it requires all 52 columns by name, carries `oz-eb5-assets.csv` verified against its manifest, and refuses an asset whose class disagrees with its tract. The page gained incentive badges, the ACS reading at designation against the latest, a per-tract asset count, two list switches (basis boost, has subsidized properties), an Assets tab and a fifth route. ### What the classes do not say

A tract class is a screen and the statute tests the site. IIUSA's one-place-per-tract observation has an unpublished method; the site test is the independent check of the 20,000 leg, and the OMB county join is an independent source for the MSA leg rather than a confirmation of IIUSA on every row. USCIS treatment of CDPs, New England towns and consolidated cities under the 20,000 test is unsettled: a 20,000+ CDP, a consolidated city-county or "(balance)" entry, a New England town at or above 20,000 at the tested interior point, and every Connecticut tract are all flagged `rural_interpretation_dependent` with the reason on the row, never silently decided either way. No row establishes availability, ownership, a listing, a permit, financing or a TEA letter. The 2027 designation round is a different list on 2020 boundaries and is not in this table.

- TIGERweb no longer serves 2010 tract geometry. HUD's FeatureServer is the source of designated 2010 polygons; point queries need JSON geometry.
- IRS 2027 appendix: 25,332 nomination-eligible tracts on 2020 boundaries, prepared locally as `.local/national-oz-appendix-import/oz-2027-eligible.jsonl`, not imported; its rural flag is the OZ definition, not the EB-5 one.
