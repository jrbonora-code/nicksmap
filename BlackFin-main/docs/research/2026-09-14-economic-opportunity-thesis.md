# Economic opportunity research: September 14, 2026

Status: initial documented research and proposed screening priorities. This is not a validated investment model, a list of available deals, or a claim that new application workflows are implemented.

## Research mandate

BlackFin should discover new revenue streams by understanding what current economic conditions cause property owners, lenders, investors and institutions to need. New opportunities must identify a potential payer, a problem, a proposed service or transaction, and the evidence needed to determine whether it works.

Evidence-backed direction was authorized on September 14. This document records the research supporting that direction and what would change it. Application requirements are in [STRATEGY-EXECUTION.md](../STRATEGY-EXECUTION.md). Earlier [source research](../rebuild/PUBLIC-DATA-RESEARCH.md) and [data-to-analysis planning](../rebuild/DATA-TO-ANALYSIS-PLAN.md) remain relevant to acquisition coverage; their historical implementation status is superseded by the current handoff.

## Working thesis and counterevidence

**Inference:** refinancing deadlines, slow lease-up and capital constraints can create demand for paid financial and development execution. Some assets will require new capital or revised ownership; others can refinance normally or benefit from improving absorption. Investigate specific decision points rather than predicting a universal crash.

The earlier concept discussion emphasized first-quarter delinquency increases. The newer second-quarter MBA release reports declines in some loan channels and increases in others [E03]. The maturity survey also reports a smaller scheduled maturity total than in 2025 [E01]. These facts weaken an assumption that distress is uniformly accelerating.

National apartment demand has exceeded completions, while rent growth remains modest [E02]. Tampa reports declining annual effective rents but improving occupancy and a moderating delivery pipeline [E04]. This combination supports researching both financing pressure and successful transitions from construction to permanent financing. A national average cannot establish conditions at a particular Florida property.

This research does not establish that current Treasury yields or all commercial mortgage rates are "skyrocketing," nor that the next Federal Reserve decision will be a hike. Policy rates, Treasury yields, SOFR, lender spreads and capitalization rates are separate observations. An investment should be tested under multiple dated financing and exit assumptions rather than made contingent on a single macro forecast.

## Research priorities and commercial hypotheses

Order below is a proposed order for learning, based on ability to test a concrete need and limit initial commitment. It is not a calibrated score, a promised return ranking or a suitability judgment. No actual customer engagement has been established.

| Lane | Economic trigger and potential payer | Proposed revenue and exposure | First practical test; evidence that rejects it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Financing transition / paid assessment | A borrower approaching construction takeout, an extension deadline or a refinancing decision [E01, E06]; owner or lender may be the client. | Fixed-scope analysis/execution fee if contracted. Staff cost, collection risk and professional liability remain; transaction compensation and authority need review. | Build a document-based options comparison for one permissioned property, including ordinary refinance and do-nothing alternatives. Reject if no solvable need, payer or feasible takeout; do not force a rescue structure where normal financing works. |
| 2. Completion and lease-up execution | A project has identifiable funded work or leasing milestones; an owner, lender or capital partner needs an execution team [E04, E06]. | Development/oversight fees and possibly contingent participation. Completion guarantees and working capital are separate negotiated exposures. | Reconcile cost-to-complete, remaining funds, permits, rent roll and schedule. Reject when the budget remains unfunded, operating demand is inadequate or the client cannot pay. |
| 3. Recapitalization / replacement sponsor | Verified shortfall plus viable post-restructuring operations; existing owner, lender and incoming investors must agree [E01, E05]. | Negotiated fee and/or equity. Identify exactly whose money is invested and who guarantees what. Reputation does not fund a shortfall. | Compare continued ownership, negotiated restructuring and sale on consistent assumptions. Reject if new money merely finances ongoing losses, required concessions are unavailable or authority/consent cannot be obtained. |
| 4. Affordability and financing structure | A property's actual rents and lender options may support workforce preservation [E07, E12]. Owner, institutional partner or eligible project entity may pay. | Contracted feasibility/development work; potential partnership only after review. Restrictions, deadlines and compliance have costs. | Compare with/without a program using dated rules, verified unit/rent scope, lender quote and full restriction period. Reject if benefits are unavailable or fail to offset costs and constraints. |
| 5. Institutional or employer housing | A specific employer/institution has documented housing demand, land, budget or procurement intent [E13]. | Paid feasibility, development management or a negotiated long-term interest. No assumption the institution supplies all capital or guarantees occupancy. | Identify a real public solicitation or documented institutional requirement and make a feasibility brief. Reject absent authority, budget, demand or a viable site/capital structure. |
| 6. Lender services / outside investor execution | A lender or investor needs local assessment, monitoring or asset execution [E14]; demand from private lenders remains unverified. | Assessment and oversight contracts, potentially recurring. Procurement, conflicts, staffing and responsibility limits matter. | Define one limited deliverable and establish a prospective payer through later authorized engagement. Reject if incumbents cover the need, entry requirements are prohibitive or scope liability defeats the fee. |
| 7. Loan/property purchases and participations | A seller offers an asset/loan at terms that can support a verified investment case; transaction availability is unverified. | Investment income and possibly separate management fees; capital at risk is explicit. | Verify availability, title/loan rights, collateral, economics and an executable capital source. Reject discounts measured only against old valuations, incomplete collateral or returns requiring unsupported rate cuts/rent growth. |

Keep an open discovery queue for other mechanisms: assumption of favorable existing financing, expiring affordability agreements, entitlement/site partnerships, resilience improvements with measurable operating benefits, and publicly funded program administration. These are unvalidated research questions until current rules, a real need, counterparties and economics are documented. Do not add them as preapproved strategies or automatically broaden the application beyond its Florida multifamily scope.

## Scenarios that could change the direction

| Scenario | Expected effect to test | Research response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rates fall and leasing improves | More borrowers can refinance; the urgency and pricing power of rescue services may fall. | Test ordinary takeout, financing transitions and selective acquisition; reduce distressed-pipeline assumptions. |
| Rates remain elevated and rents are flat | Refinancing capacity may remain constrained despite occupied properties. | Test actual obligations, lender terms, cash runway and feasible restructuring. |
| Rates/costs rise or local rents weaken | More pressure, but also more economically unrepairable assets. | Tighten feasibility and funded-reserve requirements; do not equate more distress with better investment opportunities. |
| Supply falls while demand remains durable | Some submarkets may support future housing delivery or institutional partnerships. | Test forward pipeline, replacement costs and funded demand; do not assume today's weak rents persist indefinitely. |

At a minimum, compare base/adverse/improving cases with dated inputs. Scenario labels are assumptions, not probabilities. Policy and lender eligibility remain separate from the workspace's investment thresholds.

## Missing evidence and next research packet

1. **Local conditions:** separate Tampa Bay, Sarasota/Manatee, Charlotte/Lee and Collier using comparable definitions and dates. Add concessions, collections, expenses, insurance quotations, taxes and competing deliveries where reliable. E04 alone does not cover the corridor.
2. **Debt and deadlines:** reconcile recorded instruments to current statements, modifications, extension tests, interest-rate protection and collateral. Do not manufacture an actual payoff or available transaction from a recorder entry.
3. **Operating mandate:** current services the operator wants to sell, minimum worthwhile fee, staffing, lending mandate, capital availability, guarantee limits and conflicts. These facts are unknown; neutral public research can continue.
4. **Commercial validation:** name prospective payer types and actual open procurement channels; distinguish a public requirement from a purchased service. No outreach is authorized by this document.
5. **Property evidence:** acquire permissioned operating/financing documents for any case expected to produce a decision. Preserve rejected and ordinary-refinance cases to test selection bias.

Proposed first research cohort: up to ten source-qualified Florida multifamily candidates drawn from existing coverage, including at least two ordinary-financing or non-distress comparison cases where source coverage permits. Ten is an analyst workload limit, not a validated sample size. Report the denominator, missing coverage, rejected leads and inability to classify; do not fill the cohort with invented signals. This cohort has not yet been assembled or underwritten.

## Research maintenance and acceptance

- Each decision-useful claim must carry source, observation period, geography/entity scope, retrieval date, evidence class and material limitations. Retain bounded supporting excerpts/locations or permitted source snapshots privately; do not redistribute complete copyrighted reports in Git.
- Refresh market releases when a new period is published and before a decision packet uses them. Refresh program terms and quotes for the actual transaction. These are proposed review rules, not scheduled jobs.
- A working hypothesis must name supporting evidence, counterevidence, unresolved assumptions, the next verification step and a rejection condition. No confidence percentage without calibration.
- A recommendation must identify payer, role, authority, revenue terms, costs, cash/guarantee exposure and feasible alternatives. Unknown terms remain unknown rather than becoming assumed market fees.
- Financial outputs use deterministic, versioned calculations with units and dated assumptions, subject to [calculation validation](../CALCULATION-VALIDATION.md). This documentation adds no calculation implementation.
- Implementation is accepted only after a real source-to-result-to-evidence walkthrough. A green build establishes engineering checks, not economic validity or customer demand.
