# Public data acquisition research for the BlackFin rebuild

Research date: **2026-09-12**. Status: **planning and source verification only**. No connector, application dataset, scheduled ingestion, account registration, purchase, or deployment was created by this research.

## Conclusion

A useful free-first platform is feasible. The strongest starting combination is **Florida parcel polygons and assessment/sales records + disclosed multifamily loans + state apartment licensing/inspection records**, linked on a high-quality map. Rates, bank financials, housing demand and program geography add analytical context. They must not substitute for the property and loan dataset.

The free sources do **not** establish a complete Florida loan tape, current property NOI, actual collections, current debt balances for every loan, sponsor liquidity, purchase availability, or reliable market cap rates. Those gaps need visible coverage measures and later permissioned evidence. An investment research platform can still be valuable before every field exists; filling those fields with assumptions would defeat its purpose.

The implementation order below is a proposal for the rebuild, not a record of completed work. Recommendations about identity, storage, calculations and acceptance are our engineering judgments. Source facts are linked separately.

### Explicit multifamily universe definition

Do not silently define “multifamily” as only code `003`. That captures 10+ units, while smaller multifamily sits in other categories; the published DOR strata distinguish 2–9 units and 10+ units. The target commercial apartment universe should be configurable, with an explicit decision for 5–9 units, mixed-use, manufactured housing, seniors and student housing. Condominium parcel collections are not automatically one apartment investment. [DOR submission definitions](https://floridarevenue.com/property/Documents/2024FINALCompSubmStd.pdf)

## Proposed free-first acquisition sequence

1. **Prove source contracts before designing populated screens.** Version the source registry with owner, official URL, specific dataset/version, requested access method, licence/terms evidence, expected cadence, source period, permitted retention, parsing contract and stop conditions. Count accessible sources separately from integrated sources. No paid accounts or broad scraping assumptions.
2. **Build the mapped property universe.** Acquire FDOR polygons and NAL/SDF snapshots; reconcile every county against a canonical 67-county registry with a validated DOR-to-FIPS crosswalk. Keep unknown owner/unit fields and conflicts. Include a stated multifamily inclusion policy. Retain county and municipal jurisdiction coverage rather than colouring all Florida “covered” because boundaries load.
3. **Add immediately obtainable apartment evidence.** Ingest all seven DBPR district files, then dated inspection and owner-change extracts. Deduplicate by documented source identifiers; link provisionally to parcels using address/geometry/identity evidence. Expose unmatched and ambiguous groups. Validate a stratified set of matches before enabling cross-source statistics.
4. **Deliver the first loan-linked slice.** Resolve ordinary authorised access to HUD active/terminated mortgage files, parse the actual workbook and published SOA codes, filter applicable multifamily categories and match projects to parcels. Keep excluded, missing, terminated and ambiguous rows visible in coverage accounting. If acquisition remains blocked, report it explicitly; DBPR does not become a substitute loan feed. Add Fannie/Freddie public disclosures only after exact access/terms and matching tests.
5. **Add dated rates and bank research.** Treasury and NY Fed provide financing references; FDIC supports separate lender/peer analysis. Display the last observation/reporting date and source lag. Loan-to-bank joins require lender-identity evidence; no geographic guesses. Import same-vintage peers so charts do not compare mismatched quarters.
6. **Build comparison cohorts and scenarios.** Acquire ACS/FMR/BPS/QCEW and RHFS with actual keys/downloads. Link by verified geographic vintages; source financial definitions and uncertainty must travel with every benchmark. Subject NOI and debt terms remain source observations or labelled assumptions. Only a sufficiently evidenced property can produce an actual debt-service/refinance-gap analysis; otherwise offer a clearly named sensitivity scenario with missing evidence exposed.
7. **Expand legal-event and pipeline coverage county by county.** Start with genuinely available clerk bulk sources and official property-specific document retrieval. Create a per-jurisdiction matrix for index access, images, history, update lag, terms, parse success and identifier precision. Add local permit/entitlement and collector records on the same basis. This work scales to all 67 counties without pretending that every feed already exists.
8. **Evaluate whether the platform finds useful opportunities.** Build a labelled investigation/outcome library with a cutoff date and the data actually available at that time. Separately assess evidence completeness, financing pressure, operating variance and strategy fit. Calibrated probabilities or aggregate grades require held-out evaluation; early rule flags must reveal inputs and thresholds.

## Data and map acceptance criteria

These are proposed testable release gates, not completed checks or claims that every gate needs a new framework.

- **Real ingestion:** raw source retrieval succeeds; MIME/content and schema are validated; record count, checksum, source vintage and retrieval time are recorded. A login/error HTML response, malformed ZIP, timeout or empty response must fail the run without deleting the prior successful snapshot.
- **Coverage:** a 67-row county inventory reports property-source vintage, expected and accepted counts, rejects, unmapped fields and missing geometry. Loan, clerk, inspection, zoning and benchmark coverage have independent denominators. Zero eligible records is distinguished from not fetched/not covered/parse failure.
- **Identity:** exact parcel/source IDs preserve formatting. DOR, DBPR, Census, HUD, bank and company identifiers use separate namespaces. Multi-parcel properties and cross-collateralised loans are supported. Ambiguous name/address matches never silently become confirmed ownership or sponsor control; every accepted link can be explained and reversed.
- **Geometry:** source CRS is stored and transformations verified. Test multipart polygons, holes, self-intersections, coastline/island parcels, adjacent parcels and boundary-touch cases. Analytical intersections use validated full geometry, not simplified display tiles. Compute area/distance in an appropriate metric/geographic method, not unqualified Web Mercator degrees.
- **Map/table parity:** identical filter state produces identical property sets and counts. Selection remains stable after zoom, sort, filter, pagination and reload. Multi-parcel selection highlights all relevant geometry. Unknown locations do not receive a random point or county-centre marker disguised as a property.
- **Map quality:** demonstrate statewide overview, metropolitan search, neighbourhood comparison and individual parcel selection using real data at the intended desktop viewport. Validate label hierarchy, symbol collisions, layer opacity, source legend, accessible focus and coherent basemap/aerial switching. Set and measure interaction/frame/query budgets against an agreed real dataset before accepting performance. A screenshot alone does not establish usable mapping.
- **Spatial evidence:** selecting any flood/program/plan/benchmark layer shows its publisher, effective/observation/retrieval dates, coverage, scale/precision and decisive limitations. Aerial date varies by footprint. No failed/missing layer is silently presented as “no restriction.”
- **Document truth:** confirm a stratified sample against original official records, including current and released/modified debt, unresolved and resolved inspections, name changes and multi-property collateral. Store extraction page/field evidence; uncertain OCR stays unresolved. Match review measures both false positives and missed matches.
- **Financial truth:** make units, periods and raw versus estimated values explicit. Benchmark inputs do not fill subject actuals. Refinance sensitivity uses versioned spread/coupon/NOI/cap-rate assumptions; source-reported metrics retain definitions. Independent reference cases are required before users rely on outputs.
- **Refresh and retention:** simulate revised source data, deleted/redacted record, schema change, delayed release, duplicate batch, expired key, interrupted import and recovery. Keep a recoverable history where permitted; propagate lawful redactions/restrictions. Sensitive source files stay outside Git/public assets and require the project's verified backup/access controls.

## Public-data ceiling that the product must show

The free-first screen can identify **documented facts and research leads**: apartments, ownership observations, disclosed loan maturities, observed licensing/inspection events, bank concentration, housing supply/demand context, and potential spatial programme matches.

It cannot generally determine, without additional evidence: current T12/rent roll and collections; concessions and bad debt; actual maintenance backlog/capex; insurance renewal terms; every loan's payoff, recourse, covenants, rate-cap expiry and modification status; beneficial ownership/control; available sponsor capital; willingness to sell/recapitalise; or a lender's willingness to transact. Those limitations are field-level gaps, not an excuse to revert to a manual task manager or to invent a grade.

No exhaustive audit of all county clerk/tax/zoning systems was completed in this planning pass. The next source-validation milestone must produce that jurisdiction registry and the real-data evidence above before “statewide loan coverage” or “investment-grade analytics” is claimed.
